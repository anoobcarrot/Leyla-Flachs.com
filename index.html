A little about me

Designing worlds.
Creating experiences.

I specialise in UI design, level design, gameplay, and systems design. I graduated in 2025 with a Bachelor's degree in Game Development (Game Design), and I'm excited to keep learning and gaining experience in the industry. I primarily work in Unity, with previous experience using Unreal Engine as well.

Throughout my studies, I created a variety of games, but I'm especially drawn to narrative-driven experiences. I love interactive storytelling and designing worlds that players can connect with, understand, and feel part of.

Outside of making games, I love playing them! Some of my favourites include story-rich worlds like Baldur's Gate 3 and Cyberpunk 2077. I also enjoy competitive games like Marvel Rivals, particularly its creative, interactive level design.

A story-driven climbing game · Unity

Breaking Point

Every step tests your strength. Every choice tests your trust.

Play on itch.io ↗

Overview

A promise at the top of the world.

In Breaking Point, you step into the shoes of Alice, a serious, determined climber who wants to fulfill a promise with the person she loves most: her husband, Phillip, who is climbing alongside her. Witty and reassuring, Phillip brings lightness to even the harshest moments of their journey.

Together, they face the brutal beauty of the world's tallest mountain, where every step tests more than their physical limits.

As the altitude rises, so do the stakes. A past mistake has fractured Alice's confidence, leaving her dependent on Phillip's steady voice to guide her forward. Players must navigate both the treacherous path ahead and Alice's inner struggle with self-doubt and trust.

Breaking Point is more than survival. It's a story about connection, resilience, and the unspoken battles we carry within us.

Features & design focus

  • Story-driven 3D climbing experience set on Mount Everest
  • Character-focused narrative about connection, self-doubt, resilience, and trust
  • Gameplay and level design shaped around a dangerous mountain ascent
  • Player-facing interface and scripted narrative moments

My contributions

I worked across design, implementation, UI, and team coordination, helping bring the game's mechanics and player-facing systems together into a cohesive experience.

UI & interface design

  • Designed the game's UI, including the main menu and settings menus
  • Created dialogue UI, tips UI, keybinds, and settings for options such as volume
  • Designed UI animations and the journal / quest log
  • Built the oxygen and energy interface to communicate important player resources

Gameplay systems & programming

  • Worked on the dialogue system and branching dialogue trees
  • Implemented the quick-time event (QTE) system
  • Developed the inventory system, save/load mechanic, and level-loading flow
  • Worked on the day-and-night system and player-death flow
  • Helped connect the game's mechanics so the systems worked together
  • Synced player animations with movement

Audio, cinematics & level design

  • Implemented music and sound effects using FMOD
  • Worked on cinematic sequences and some level design
  • Contributed to bug fixing and overall polish

Writing & team collaboration

  • Contributed to script writing and narrative implementation
  • Helped manage the team and coordinate work across different areas of development

These responsibilities span both creative design and technical implementation, from shaping the player experience through menus and feedback to connecting gameplay systems and supporting the final polish.
